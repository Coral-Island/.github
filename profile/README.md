### 1. A Living, Breathing Island  
From dense jungles to sparkling beaches, Coral Island is a world that feels alive. Seasons change, wildlife thrives, and the island evolves based on your actions.  

### 2. Farming with a Tropical Twist  
Grow unique crops like coconuts 🥥, pineapples 🍍, and dragon fruit. Raise animals such as alpacas, chickens, and even **friendly capybaras**!
